Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2025

VeeraLyse ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our AI-powered YouTube video analysis platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you use VeeraLyse, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we access your name, email address, and profile picture to create and manage your account.
  • YouTube Data: With your explicit permission, we access your YouTube channel data including channel statistics, video metadata, video analytics, and comments to power the YouTube Studio features.
  • Video Files: Videos you upload for analysis are processed by our AI system and temporarily stored during analysis. They are not permanently retained after analysis is complete.
  • Analysis Results: AI-generated analysis results may be stored in your account history if you are signed in.

2. How We Use YouTube Data

VeeraLyse uses YouTube API Services to provide the following features:

  • Display your YouTube channel statistics and video library
  • Show video analytics (views, watch time, impressions, CTR)
  • Allow editing of video metadata (title, description, tags)
  • Display and manage video comments
  • Upload videos to your YouTube channel with AI-generated metadata

By using VeeraLyse, you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Our use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. How We Store Your Data

  • Your analysis history is stored securely in Google Firestore, linked to your Google account.
  • Uploaded video files are processed temporarily and deleted after analysis.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.
  • YouTube access tokens are used only during your active session and are not stored permanently on our servers.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect our rights and the safety of our users
  • With service providers (Firebase, Google Cloud) who assist in operating our platform under strict confidentiality agreements

5. Revoking Access

You can revoke VeeraLyse's access to your Google account and YouTube data at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions and removing VeeraLyse from the list of connected apps.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data and analysis history for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure cloud storage, and access controls.

8. Children's Privacy

VeeraLyse is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date.
